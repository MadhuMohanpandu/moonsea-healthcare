Company Overview

Integrated Healthcare Solutions Under One Roof

Moonsea Private Limited (moonsea.in) is an integrated healthcare company operating across manufacturing, medical equipment procurement, turnkey hospital infrastructure setup, pharmaceutical distribution, and healthcare advisory.

Our goal is to support healthcare institutions, hospitals, diagnostic centers, and clinics by supplying high-performance surgical devices, cleanroom operation theatre engineering, and uninterrupted maintenance services.

Our Mission

Provide affordable, high-quality medical infrastructure and healthcare products to empower healthcare organizations across India.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted partner for turnkey hospital infrastructure, surgical supplies, and healthcare distribution nationwide.
