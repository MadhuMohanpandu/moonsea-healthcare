About MOONSEA ENTERPRISES PRIVATE LIMITED

Incorporated on 27 September 2024, MOONSEA ENTERPRISES PRIVATE LIMITED is a registered private non-government healthcare and pharmaceutical retail enterprise headquartered in Nellore, Andhra Pradesh.

Retail Sale of Pharmaceuticals & Orthopaedic Goods

MOONSEA ENTERPRISES PRIVATE LIMITED operates primarily in the retail sale of pharmaceuticals, medical and orthopaedic goods, and toilet articles.

Our business model emphasizes quality assurance, genuine pharmaceutical procurement, customer accessibility, and strict adherence to healthcare regulations under the Registrar of Companies (RoC - Vijayawada).

Our Mission

To provide accessible, affordable, and authentic pharmaceutical and orthopaedic goods to local communities and healthcare institutions.

Our Vision

To expand our retail presence and franchise network across Andhra Pradesh and South India through sustainable healthcare retail practices.

Our Core Commitments

Key operational principles guiding our pharmaceutical and retail activities.

Authentic Products

Direct sourcing from verified pharmaceutical manufacturers and authorized distributors.

Patient-Centric Care

Ensuring affordable pricing, prescription guidance, and reliable customer service.

Corporate Integrity

Full statutory compliance under CIN U47721AP2024PTC116151 with RoC Vijayawada.
